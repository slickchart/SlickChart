Privacy Policy

SlickChart · Effective date: October 8, 2026

Who we are: SlickChart (“SlickChart,” “we,” “us”) is a practice-management app operated by SlickChart, LLC, a California limited liability company, Pleasant Hill, CA. Questions: support@slickchart.app.

1. Overview

SlickChart helps beauty and wellness professionals (“Providers”) manage their practice — client charts, appointments, forms, photos, product recommendations, messaging, and payments. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

There are two kinds of people in SlickChart, and it matters which one you are:

  • Providers — the professionals who sign up for and use SlickChart.
  • Clients — the customers of those Providers, whose information a Provider enters into SlickChart.

For information a Provider enters about their Clients, the Provider is responsible for that information and for having the right to store it (see Section 6). SlickChart processes it on the Provider’s behalf.

2. Information we collect

  • a. Provider account information. Your name, email address, a securely hashed password, business details you enter (business name, hours, branding), and language preference.
  • b. Client information you (the Provider) enter. This may include a Client’s name, contact details, birthday, appointment history, treatment notes, intake-form answers, and — depending on how you use SlickChart — skin/health details, allergies, contraindications, medications you note, and photos. You control what you enter.
  • c. Payment information. Your SlickChart subscription is billed through Stripe, and any Client payments you choose to take run through Square (when you connect it) — each entered and processed on their own secure systems. SlickChart never sees, handles, or stores full card numbers.
  • d. Usage and technical information. Basic technical data needed to run the app (for example, log data and error reports). We do not sell this.
  • e. Feedback. If you submit feedback, we store your message and rating (and your account email) so we can improve the product.

3. How we use information

We use information to: provide and operate the app; authenticate your account; sync your Square data when you connect it; send transactional emails (verification, password resets); send product updates if you opt in; respond to feedback and support; keep the service secure; and comply with law.

We do not sell your information or your Clients’ information. We do not use Client health-adjacent information for advertising.

4. AI features

Some SlickChart features can use AI to draft suggestions (for example, note or message drafts) or to transcribe voice notes. These features are optional and can be turned off in Settings. When used, the relevant text is sent to Anthropic (Claude) to generate a response, and any voice-note audio you record is sent to Groq for transcription. Do not rely on AI output as medical advice.

5. Service providers (subprocessors)

We use trusted companies to run SlickChart. Each receives only what it needs:

  • Vercel — application hosting.
  • Neon — the database that stores your account and the data you enter.
  • Stripe — processing your SlickChart subscription payments.
  • Square — if you connect it, syncing your clients/appointments/catalog and processing any Client payments you take through your own Square account.
  • Resend — sending transactional and (opt-in) newsletter emails.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — generating optional AI suggestions when you use those features.
  • Groq — transcribing voice notes to text when you use that feature.
  • Google Firebase (FCM) and Apple (APNs) — delivering push notifications to the mobile apps.

We will keep this list current. If we add a subprocessor that materially changes how data is handled, we’ll update this policy.

6. Google Calendar (if you connect it)

Connecting Google Calendar is entirely optional, and nothing below happens unless you choose to connect it. You can disconnect at any time from Sync Calendar inside SlickChart, or revoke access directly in your Google Account under Security → Third-party access.

What we ask Google for. One permission only: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events. This lets SlickChart see and change events on your calendars. It does not let us create or delete calendars, change who a calendar is shared with, or read anything else in your Google Account.

What we use it for. Exactly two user-facing features, both visible on the Sync Calendar screen:

  • Your SlickChart appointments appear in Google. When you add, change or cancel an appointment in SlickChart, we create, update or delete the matching event on your calendar.
  • Your own calendar blocks client bookings. When a Client opens your booking page, we read the events on your calendar for the day they are looking at, so a time you are already busy is not offered. Events you have marked Free in Google are ignored, which is how Google defines that setting.

What SlickChart sends to Google. For each appointment you book in SlickChart we send the date and time, how long it runs, the Client's name and the service name (together they become the event title, for example “Jen Marsh — Facial”), and any short booking note you typed on that appointment. That is all. Treatment notes, session summaries, intake forms, photos, body maps, product plans and health information are never sent to Google. If you would rather your Clients' names not appear in Google Calendar, do not connect it.

What we store. Only what those two features need:

  • The access and refresh tokens Google issues, so the connection keeps working without you signing in again.
  • A list matching each SlickChart appointment to the Google event we created for it, so that editing the appointment updates the same event instead of making a duplicate.
  • Whether the last sync succeeded, so the app can tell you if it needs reconnecting.

When we read your calendar to block booking times, we use only the start and end times of your events, and we work them out at the moment the booking page is opened. We do not store the titles, descriptions, locations, guests, or any other contents of your existing Google events. Nothing we read from your calendar is shown to your Clients; they only see which times are unavailable.

Limited Use. SlickChart's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically, we do not transfer or sell Google user data to third parties such as advertising platforms, data brokers or information resellers; we do not use it for advertising of any kind, including retargeting or personalized advertising; we do not use it to determine credit-worthiness or for lending; and no human reads your Google calendar data except where you specifically ask us to help with a problem, where it is needed for security, or where the law requires it.

When you disconnect. We delete the stored tokens and the appointment-to-event list. Events already on your Google Calendar stay there, because they are yours; delete any you do not want in Google itself.

7. Providers’ responsibilities for Client data

If you are a Provider, you are responsible for:

  • Having the legal right and any necessary consent to store your Clients’ information and photos in SlickChart.
  • Telling your Clients, as required by law, how their information is used.
  • Keeping your login secure and only entering information you’re permitted to.

You are the party that decides what Client information to collect and why. SlickChart acts as your service provider for that data.

8. How we protect information

We use reasonable safeguards, including encryption of data in transit, hashed passwords, and encryption of connected Square access tokens. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you of a breach as required by law.

9. Data retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service. You can permanently delete your data from within the app at any time: Providers go to Settings → Delete account, and Clients go to Privacy & Data → Delete my data. This performs a real server-side deletion — the account/record and its associated data are removed, connected Square tokens are revoked, and any subscription is cancelled. You can also email support@slickchart.app to request deletion. We delete or de-identify data within a reasonable period, except where we must keep certain records to comply with law, and some information may remain in backups for a limited time.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of marketing. To exercise these rights, email support@slickchart.app.

California residents: Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended), you have rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of “sharing” of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, contact us at the email above; we will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

11. Email preferences

Transactional emails (verification, password resets) are required to use the service. Marketing/product-update emails are opt-in and every one includes an unsubscribe link.

12. Children

SlickChart is for professionals and is not directed to children under 18. Do not create an account if you are under 18. Providers are responsible for any legal requirements that apply when a Client is a minor.
