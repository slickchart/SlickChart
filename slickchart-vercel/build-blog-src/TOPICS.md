# Build blog: the topic queue

The weekly Routine takes the next unwritten topic from this list, writes it as a `draft: true` post,
and reports it for review. When one is published, tick it. Add new ones freely.

The list is ordered roughly by search intent: the earlier ones are what people type before they've
committed to anything, the later ones are for people mid-build.

## Queue

- [ ] **What to build: the shape of an idea that works.** Careful framing — `/build` says it does not
      help you find an idea, so this is about *recognising* whether the one you have is the right
      size, not generating one. One job, one person, done weekly by hand.
- [ ] **The first seven days.** What to actually do in week one, including the slow things to start
      immediately because they finish on their own.
- [ ] **How to check an app when you cannot read code.** Use it like a person. Try to break it. What
      "tested" means for someone non-technical.
- [ ] **The four safety mistakes that cover most of the risk.** Written for someone who is about to
      charge money and store other people's information. No scaremongering.
- [ ] **Why your app needs a privacy policy and terms before you submit anywhere.** Real URLs, real
      answers that match the store forms.
- [ ] **Do you need an LLC to sell an app?** Honest, non-legal-advice version: what it is for, what
      it is not, look up your own state, never pay hundreds for a form.
- [ ] **What to do when the store turns you down.** Rejection is normal, it comes with a reason code,
      and it is usually a small fix.
- [ ] **Six habits that keep your AI bill down.** One change at a time, boundaries, new conversations,
      don't paste the whole app.
- [ ] **Launching to nobody: the founding offer.** Why launch work starts in week two and not on
      launch day.
- [ ] **What breaks after launch.** Renewals, filings and certificates that quietly take an app
      offline if nobody wrote them in a calendar.
- [ ] **Building between clients.** Time management for someone with a real job. Honest about six
      weeks meaning evenings.
- [ ] **What I would do differently.** The five things that cost real time.

## Topics to avoid

- Anything that names a dollar figure someone could earn.
- "AI will replace developers" takes. Not the audience, not the argument, and it ages badly.
- Comparisons to other build-an-app courses by name. Nothing to gain, and their pricing changes.
- Tutorials that duplicate the roadmap step by step. The blog argues the *why*; the product is the
  ordered *how*. A post that hands over the sequence for free removes the reason to buy it.
