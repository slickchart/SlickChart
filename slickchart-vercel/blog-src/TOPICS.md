## Strategy behind this list

Researched against how GlossGenius, Vagaro, Mangomint, Square, Fresha, Boulevard and Comb win traffic,
and what actually makes a beauty pro buy or switch software.

**What works for them, that we copy:** comparison and "alternatives" pages (highest purchase intent in
the category — GlossGenius ranks for *Vagaro vs Mangomint*, a comparison it isn't even in), niche
software pages per profession, and honest total-cost breakdowns.

**What we do better — the three things no competitor can say:**

1. **We don't ask you to leave Square.** Every competitor is a replacement: their content has to argue
   you should rip out your booking and POS. Research on why people *don't* switch is unanimous — the
   blocker is migration, retraining, and rebuilding integrations. SlickChart connects to the seller's
   own Square, so the pitch is "keep what works, add what's missing." Nobody else can run that play.
2. **Charting is the product, not a tier.** Booking apps bolt notes on; real charting (photo markup,
   storage, compliance) tends to sit behind a pricier medical/clinical tier. Ours is the core.
3. **We collapse the stack.** PMU and waxing pros routinely pay for a booking app *plus* a separate
   consent-form app. One place for notes, photos, forms and aftercare removes a whole subscription.

**Buying triggers to write toward** (what makes people actually switch): reliability and downtime;
how many taps a task costs on a busy day; support that's a chatbot; add-on pricing and processing
rates that weren't obvious up front; and fear of migration.

**Audience order:** estheticians first, then waxing, PMU, and makeup artists.
