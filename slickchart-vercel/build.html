Timing that knows your services

  • Fill and touch-up windows, where the price depends on time since the last visit.
  • Processing time you can book into, so you take someone else while color processes.
  • Add-ons that change time and price, so nail art or extra rows never overlap your day.
  • Minimum time between visits, and follow-ups booked for you: touch-ups, perfecting sessions, downsizes.

Checks before you start

  • Patch tests that hold a booking until a valid test is on file.
  • Consent signed every visit, in your wording, never editable afterwards.
  • ID and age checks, a medical exam on file, or a surgeon's clearance, where your work needs them.

Records shaped to your craft

  • A color formula card, a lash map, a pigment record from your own stock, a face map with lots and units, SOAP notes, or a jewelry record.
  • Before and after photos, private, side by side, with separate permission for your marketing.

Care that runs itself

  • Rebooking reminders on your schedule, and aftercare messages on the days you pick.
  • Home-care plans, packages and memberships, waitlists, and moving your clients over from your old app.

Some of my services have three parts: application, processing, and finishing. During processing I am free to take another client, but the chair stays held.

In {your app}, let me set the minutes for each part, per service. During processing, let other bookings fit only if they fit inside that gap and do not need the same chair.

Keep this separate from clean-up time, which blocks me completely. Mixing up the two is how stylists get double-booked.

Questions people ask

I am not a tech person. Can I really do this?
I am not one either. I am an esthetician. You will not write code. You will describe what you want, copy the prompt the roadmap gives you, paste it, and check that it works, one step at a time. If you can follow a treatment protocol, you can follow this. It still takes real time and attention, and you do the work.
Will it mess up my bookings while I build?
No, because you keep your current booking app the whole time. You build yours on the side, test it with yourself and a few regulars, and only move over when it works.
Can I take deposits and card payments?
Yes. The roadmap sets up deposits per service through Stripe's own payment page, so card numbers never touch your app. Card fees are about the same as you pay now, so do not expect to save on those.
What about client health info, like HIPAA?
Most estheticians, lash, nail, hair and massage pros who are paid directly by clients are not covered by HIPAA. Med spas and anyone who bills insurance can be. Either way, your clients' info is yours to protect. The roadmap keeps it private, backed up and off any public link, and never puts ad trackers on your forms. If you bill insurance or run a med spa, check with a professional before you store health records.
Can I bring my clients over from my current app?
Your client list usually exports from your booking app as a spreadsheet. Notes and visit history are harder: some apps only hand those over if you contact their support. Ask them early. Your own app has a full export built in from the start.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. And you will not learn a coding language here. You will learn how to tell Claude what to build, how to check what comes back, and what to do in what order. That is the real skill now. You do not need to know how to read or write code. You just have to be good at following directions.
Is this just a list of prompts?
No. Prompts on their own are the easy part. You can get those anywhere. The hard part is knowing which prompt to run, and in which week. That is what took me hundreds of hours. Some steps will not even work unless something else is true first. That is why the prompts sit on the steps instead of in a list.
What do I need before I start?
A Claude account on a paid plan, and a clear idea of how your business runs. That is it. The first thing the roadmap does is walk you through opening your accounts, one step at a time.

The free starter is different. That one is much smaller, and a free Claude account handles it, so you can see what this feels like before paying for anything. The full build is a different amount of work, and that is where a paid plan matters.
Is this a course with modules and hours of video?
No, and that is on purpose. There is one 22 minute video, and its whole job is to hand you the roadmap and show you how to use it. The roadmap is the product. You do not need me talking for eight hours. You need to know what to do next.
Do I have to put my app in the app stores?
No, and this surprises people. What you build works in any browser, on any phone or computer. People can add it to their home screen and it works like a normal app. The stores are one extra step, they are marked optional, and plenty of businesses never bother.
How long does it really take?
It depends on your app and how much time you have. The roadmap is laid out in weeks so you know the order, but they are a guide, not a deadline. Go at your own pace and nothing breaks.
What if I get stuck?
The roadmap has a prompt for that too. It is a way of describing what broke so Claude can actually fix it instead of guessing. Claude is your help desk here, and it is awake at two in the morning, which no person is. There is no coaching included and I want to be straight with you about that.
