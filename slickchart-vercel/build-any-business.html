Questions people ask

I am not a tech person. Can I really do this?
I am not one either. I am an esthetician. You will not write code. You will describe what you want, copy the prompt the roadmap gives you, paste it, and check that it works, one step at a time. If you can follow a recipe or a checklist, you can follow this. It still takes real time and attention, and you do the work.
Will it work for my kind of business?
The roadmap is not tied to one trade. Every prompt fills in with your app's name, who it is for and the one job it does, and Claude interviews you about your business before it builds anything. If you run a beauty business, the Beauty Biz App Build Roadmap is the better fit, because its prompts are already written for your trade.
Can I take card payments?
Yes, if you charge. The roadmap sets up payments through Stripe, sold on your website rather than inside the app, so card numbers never touch your app.
What about my customers' private information?
It is yours to protect. The roadmap has Claude test whether one person could ever see another's information, keeps keys and passwords out of the code, and has your privacy policy live before anyone else uses the app. If your business has special rules for the information it keeps, check with a professional before you store it.
Do I need to know how to code?
No. And you will not learn a coding language here. You will learn how to tell Claude what to build, how to check what comes back, and what to do in what order. That is the real skill now. You do not need to know how to read or write code. You just have to be good at following directions.
Is this just a list of prompts?
No. A prompt that makes exactly what you want is the hard part, and knowing which one to run first is the other half. Each prompt here was worked out over thousands of hours on my own app, and each one sits on the step it belongs to, with directions for where to paste it and what to check. Some steps will not even work unless something else is true first. That is why the prompts sit on the steps instead of in a list.
What do I need before I start?
A Claude account on a paid plan, and a clear idea of how your business runs. That is it. The first thing the roadmap does is walk you through opening your accounts, one step at a time.

The free starter is different. That one is much smaller, and a free Claude account handles it, so you can see what this feels like before paying for anything. The full build is a different amount of work, and that is where a paid plan matters.
Is this a course with modules and hours of video?
No, and that is on purpose. There is one 22 minute video, and its whole job is to hand you the roadmap and show you how to use it. The roadmap is the product. You do not need me talking for eight hours. You need to know what to do next.
Do I have to put my app in the app stores?
No, and this surprises people. What you build works in any browser, on any phone or computer. People can add it to their home screen and it works like a normal app. The stores are one extra step, they are marked optional, and plenty of businesses never bother.
How long does it really take?
It depends on your app and how much time you have. The roadmap is laid out in weeks so you know the order, but they are a guide, not a deadline. Go at your own pace and nothing breaks.
What if I get stuck?
The roadmap has a prompt for that too. It is a way of describing what broke so Claude can actually fix it instead of guessing. Claude is your help desk here, and it is awake at two in the morning, which no person is. There is no coaching included and I want to be straight with you about that.
