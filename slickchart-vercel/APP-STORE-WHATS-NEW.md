# App Store — "What's New" release notes

Paste one of these into App Store Connect → your app version → **What's New in
This Version**. (Apple hides "What's New" on a brand-new app's first release, so
if this is still version 1.0 use the **Launch** copy as your description's lead
or bump to 1.0.1 and use the **Update** copy.)

---

## Update copy (for 1.0.1 and later) — ~My recommendation

```
Fresh look, same SlickChart. This release brings our finalized app icon and a
refreshed, more polished experience across the app.

• New SlickChart app icon and brand look
• Smoother first-visit flow — send a client's intake and pre-visit check-in in
  one tap, and they complete both in a single guided sequence
• Cleaner client invites — edit the message before you send it, with a quick
  confirmation every time
• Clearer home screen: see exactly who still needs a form or a first-visit
  package
• Lots of small polish and reliability fixes

As always, your clients use SlickChart free — no app to download and no login.
```
