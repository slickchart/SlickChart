All-in-one salon software or best-of-breed? How solo estheticians and beauty pros can decide. New on the SlickChart blog.

Every software choice ends at the same fork: one platform that does everything, or keep what works and add the best tool for what is missing.

What all-in-one gets you
One login, one bill, one support line, and nothing to connect. If you are just starting out with nothing in place, it can be a sensible first choice.

What best-of-breed gets you
Keep the part you love and fix the part you don't. That was my own situation. My booking runs on Square, so it is all integrated, and my clients already knew how to book me. What was missing was really good note-taking formats made specifically for my profession.

When replacing everything is the wrong move
Your booking and payments work, the problem is one part, your history might not come with you, or it is your busy season.

When all-in-one is the right call
Your booking is unreliable, you pay for tools that overlap and don't connect, or you have no history to lose.

Questions to ask first
Where does the client record live? Do your tools share clients on their own? Can you export notes, photos and forms? How many taps does your busiest task take? What will your clients notice?
