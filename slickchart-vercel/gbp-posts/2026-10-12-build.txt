Test an app you built with AI when you cannot read code. New on the Build blog.

I am an esthetician. I built my own app in six weeks, between clients, with no tech background and no coding. The worry that arrives once it starts working is always the same. I cannot read any of this, so how would I know if it is broken?

The surprise
You are better placed to test it than you think. AI writes code that runs, and running is not the same as being right. An AI app builder does not know your trade, so it will build something that works perfectly and does the wrong thing. That is not a coding problem. It is a what is this actually for problem, and you are the only one who knows the answer.

Test as a stranger
The big one, and almost nobody does it. You built the whole app already signed in, so the one path you never walked is the path every new user walks first. Sign out. Sign up from scratch. Reset your password on purpose.

The break list
Eight mean things that need no code knowledge at all. Leave a required field empty. Put an apostrophe in a name. Tap save twice, fast. Turn the wifi off mid save.

The two questions worth most
Did it actually save, and is it still there tomorrow on your other device? Both screens can say fine.

What it will not tell you
Whether your app is safe. Storing customer data is a different question and the post says so instead of pretending.
