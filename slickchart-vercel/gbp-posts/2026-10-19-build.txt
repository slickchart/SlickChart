Build your own booking app for your beauty business, without coding. New on the Build blog.

I am an esthetician. For years I paid every month for software that did most things fine and almost nothing the way my room works. If you do lashes, nails, hair, brows, massage or tattoo, you probably know the feeling.

The workarounds
Lash fills priced by time since the last visit. Patch tests that should hold a booking. Color processing time where you are free but the chair is not. Formula cards, lash maps, consent in your own wording. In a booking app built for every salon at once, all of it ends up in a notes box, a screenshot or a second app.

Why a rented app never quite fits
A company selling one app to thousands of businesses has to build for the middle. Your trade's details wait, and changes happen on their schedule, not yours.

What your own app means
Not learning to code. AI writes the code now. You decide what the app should do, describe it in plain words, and check what comes back. That is the part a beauty pro is already good at.

The honest trade offs
You are the one who checks it, especially that one client can never see another's information. You own the privacy side too. App store rules change, so check them yourself. And you may not need the app stores at all, which surprises most.
