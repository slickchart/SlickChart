Best software for a solo esthetician: an honest 2026 breakdown, now on the blog.

Most esthetician software lists rank products that are not doing the same job. A booking app and a clinical records system sit side by side, one of them wins, and you still cannot tell whether it fixes your problem. So I wrote the honest version.

What is in it?
Four kinds of esthetician software, what each is built for, and what it costs. Booking first platforms, clinical and medspa EMR systems, per seat tools with charting included, and adding charting to the booking you already have. Every price came off the company's own pricing page on 12 October 2026, not from memory.

The question that decides it
Is your booking and payment working? Not perfect. Working. If it is, then what you are short of is client charting, and replacing the half that works is a lot of upheaval.

Where SlickChart fits
SlickChart adds esthetician client charting to the Square account you already have. Your calendar, booking link and payments stay put, and clients see no change. You gain the per visit record: treatment notes, before and after photos, consent forms and aftercare.

What it is not
Not a booking system. If booking is the broken part, this will not fix it.

Who should skip it
Already have proper SOAP notes somewhere you like? Stay where you are. The post names one and says so.

Ask any software vendor two things: the card not present rate, and what an export of your own client records costs if you leave.
