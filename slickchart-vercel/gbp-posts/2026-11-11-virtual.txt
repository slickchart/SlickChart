Virtual esthetics: how a solo esthetician earns outside the treatment room. New on the SlickChart blog.

There are only so many facials one pair of hands can do in a week. I started doing virtual consults to grow my income without adding more service hours, and to stop only trading my time for money.

Virtual consults
A skin analysis consult where the client sends guided photos from their phone, or a product consult with no photos at all. Both end in a plan and product picks. Clients can be local, between visits, or many states away.

Guides and courses
Write the advice you repeat every week once, and send it in a tap. Group a few lessons into a course. My clients' favorite is my 6-week vitality reset.

Shoppable homecare plans
Every product you recommend comes with a way to buy it: in office, your own shop, a brand affiliate link, or Amazon under your own tag. My virtual clients buy only through my affiliate links, so I never hold inventory for them.

How they connect
A stranger finds your consult link in your Instagram bio. The consult ends in picks they can buy. A guide shows them how to use the new serum. A follow up photo a few weeks later shows what changed.

Keep it in one place
SlickChart is the app I built for this. Photo and product consults, a course and guide builder, and shoppable homecare plans, all on the client record. SlickChart never takes a cut of your sales.
