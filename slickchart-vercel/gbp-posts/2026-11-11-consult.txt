Virtual skin consultation: how estheticians run photo and product consults that clients act on. New on the SlickChart blog.

Every "can I send you a pic of my chin?" DM is already a consult. I started offering virtual consults to grow my income without adding more hours of service time.

Two kinds of consult
A skin analysis consult uses guided photos from the client's own phone. A product consult needs no photos, for "what should I be using?" or a routine refresh.

Ask for the right photos
Front and both sides at eye level, in daylight facing a window, with no filter and no makeup. Ask what they use now. Photos of the bottles save a lot of back and forth.

Stay in scope
A virtual consult is cosmetic advice, not a diagnosis. If something needs a doctor, say so and note it. Licensing is set state by state, so check with your board before you offer consults across state lines.

What to charge
In my own business, I either charge for the consult or let the fee count as a credit toward products. Every provider can choose what works for them.

End with something they can act on
A short plan, each product with a reason, a way to buy each one right there, and a follow up date for a photo in the same light.

Keep it on the client record
With SlickChart, clients request a consult through your link, take the photos you ask for, and get your picks in their own app. Turn on AI if you want help reading photos.
