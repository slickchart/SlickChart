Rules change
Your board updates a rule, your insurer asks for a new consent line, or a product changes its patch test instructions. All of it has to show up in how you book and check in clients.

When the software is rented
Forms are often easy to edit, but deeper rules, like a wait between two services or a question that blocks a booking, are usually features. If the feature is not there, you ask and wait, or work around it with memory and sticky notes.

When the app is yours
You describe the change in plain words: what to ask, which services it applies to, what happens if the answer is no. AI makes the change and you test it. That is exactly the kind of small, clear request AI handles well. The rule then lives in the app, not in your memory, even on your busiest Saturday.

The honest trade-off
Nobody else watches your board's updates for you. You are the one who checks. That is real responsibility.

Habits that make it easy
Bookmark your board, insurer and product pages. Keep a short list of what your app enforces and why. Change one thing at a time. Book the service yourself and try to break the rule. Keep the version each client signed.

Check your own board for the rules that apply to you, since they differ from state to state.
