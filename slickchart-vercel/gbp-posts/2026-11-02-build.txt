Build your own booking app that prices lash fills by the last visit, without coding. New on the Build blog.

Many lash booking pages list three or four fill services that are really one, with a note on which to pick based on the date of the last visit.

The rule the app cannot see
A fill is priced by how long it has been since the last appointment. Most booking apps price by the service the client picks from a menu, so the artist splits one service into several, writes instructions, and checks every booking by hand.

Not only lashes
Color root touch-ups versus a full color. Brow tints and lamination on a regular rhythm. A PMU perfecting session inside its window versus a refresh a year later. Each trade has its own version.

When the app is yours
The client books "a fill". The app looks up their last visit and shows the right fill, the right price and the right time on your calendar. Too long? It offers the full set before anyone arrives.

The hard part is deciding the rule
AI writes the code now, so you can build an app without coding. The real work is your decisions: where each window starts and ends, what happens on the boundary day, and what a brand new client sees.

Then try to break it
Book it as each client would: right on time, a day past a window, after a full set, a new client tapping "fill" by mistake. That is your app testing checklist, and none of it needs code.
