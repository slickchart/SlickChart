Build your own booking app for two trades in one visit, like skin and lashes or hair and nails, without coding. New on the Build blog.

Many beauty pros do more than one thing. I pair nutrition consults with skin treatments, treatment first, then we talk about what is happening on the inside. Most booking apps handle a pairing like that badly.

What meets in one visit
Two sets of intake questions, two sets of rules like a patch test, two timings in an order you choose, and two kinds of aftercare. A combo service hides all of it inside one name.

Rules belong to services
In your own app, each service carries its own forms, rules and aftercare. When a client books two, the visit gathers both. They answer each question once, sign each consent once, and get one aftercare list.

You decide what is safe
Whether two services belong on the same day, and in which order, is your call from your training. For me there is no pairing I refuse on the same day. Yours may differ, and your app follows your call.

The real work is writing it down
AI writes the code now. Your job is the rules, in plain words, plus the awkward cases: the second service booked a week later, or one service blocked while the other is fine.

Then test it like a client
Book each service alone, both together, both in the other order, and with no patch test on file. Check each question appears once. None of it needs code.
