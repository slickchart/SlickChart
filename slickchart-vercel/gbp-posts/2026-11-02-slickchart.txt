PMU software without three subscriptions: booking, consent forms and healed photos in one place. New on the SlickChart blog.

Many permanent makeup artists pay for a booking app and a consent form app, and keep healed photos in the camera roll. Each tool works. The trouble is the gaps between them, because a PMU client is a series: consult, patch test, first session, perfecting session, and a refresh later.

Booking that knows it is a series
Book the perfecting session before the client leaves, and see on the client record when the last session was and what was done.

Consent forms before the appointment
A health questionnaire filled in on the morning is too late to act on. Look for PMU consent forms the client fills in on their own phone days ahead, with risky answers flagged for you.

Photos that tell the healing story
Before, fresh and healed photos are your record. Keep them on the client record, backed up, taken from the same angle each time, and private unless the client signed a release.

Aftercare they can find again
Written aftercare clients can open again on day five saves a lot of worried texts.

Keep what already works
If your booking and payments already run on Square, you may not need to replace them. SlickChart is the permanent makeup software I built to add client charting, PMU consent forms, treatment notes and photos to the Square account you already have.
