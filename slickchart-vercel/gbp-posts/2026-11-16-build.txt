Moving clients to your own app is not a data migration. Clients are invited, and how that feels decides who comes with you. New on the Build blog.

Two moves in one week
Your records move: names, contact details, allergies, notes, photos and signed forms. Your clients change a habit: a new link to book. Do the records first, quietly, and invite clients only once the app already knows them.

Bring records over first
Ask your current provider in writing what you can export. A client who opens your app and sees their name, their next visit and their last aftercare feels welcome. Check allergies by hand after the move, and have a real privacy policy before anyone arrives.

No download required
When I moved my own clients onto my app, the feedback was great. The one concern I heard was from clients who did not want to be forced to download another app. So I made sure everything works from a link, with no download needed.

One link, everywhere
Change your Instagram bio, website, text signature and reminders on the same day, and keep the old booking link working for a while.

Tell your regulars yourself
A short message in your own voice: what is new, what stays the same, and that their history came with them.

Test it as a client
Open the link on your phone as if you had never seen it. Book, find your record, open your aftercare. Then ask a friend to do the same and watch them.
