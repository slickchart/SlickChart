Shoppable homecare plans: how estheticians get paid for the products they already recommend. New on the SlickChart blog.

Clients do not ignore your recommendations. They leave, forget the exact name, and buy whatever is closest. A shoppable plan puts a way to buy right next to every product you recommend.

Four ways to buy
In office, at their next visit. Your own online shop. The brand, through your affiliate link. Amazon, under your own Associates tag. Let the client choose.

What I do
In my own studio, most clients still buy in person, and some buy through my affiliate links. My virtual clients buy only through affiliate links, so I never have to hold inventory for them.

What goes in a plan
The routine in order, morning and evening. Each product with a reason. A buy option on every product. And what to stop using, which is often the most valuable line.

Disclose your links
If you earn from a link, say so. In the US the FTC expects a clear disclosure, and Amazon has its own terms. A plain line like "I may earn a commission from some of these links" is the usual approach. Check the current rules yourself.

Keep it honest
Recommend what you would anyway. Trusted picks keep selling for years.
